      First time posting?

      To foster a high quality art community on ArtStation, we ask that you please respect our posting guidelines.

      Types of art

      Generally ArtStation welcomes a wide variety of visual art related to games, film, media & entertainment. This includes concept art, illustration, matte paintings, 3D, animation, visual effects, user interfaces, animated films, sketches, sculptures and more. Take a look at what’s already been featured on ArtStation to get an idea of what the community expects.

      Only post your own work

      ArtStation is a portfolio platform. The works on your portfolio should be work that you created. Please only publish work that either you own or that you have permission to publish.

      If this is a studio profile, only post work that the studio produced. More info for studio/company profiles

      No erotic artwork

      ArtStation is not the place to post erotic artwork. Please read our guidelines.

      No photography

      ArtStation is not a photography community. We recommend 500px for that. Posting photographs of artwork (e.g. sketches, sculptures) is ok.

      No posting other people's artwork

      ArtStation is a portfolio platform. The work on your portfolio should be the work that YOU created.
